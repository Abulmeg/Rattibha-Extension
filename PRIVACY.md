# Rattibha Learn privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Rattibha Learn is an independent browser extension for JUST eLearning. This policy covers the extension, not the Rattibha website or mobile app.

## Information it uses

The extension reads the course pages you open on `elearn.just.edu.jo`, including course names, sections, materials, announcements and resource links, to show an alternative interface. When you open Chat, it accesses the conversations and messages available to your existing university account and can send the replies you choose.

The extension uses your current JUST eLearning session. It does not ask for, collect, or save your university password.

## What stays in your browser

Settings, pinned courses and resources, your tasks, recently opened resources, focus timer information and cached course data are saved in browser extension storage. Message contents are loaded for display and are not intentionally saved to that persistent storage.

Files you choose to convert are processed in your browser. They are not sent to a separate Rattibha Learn conversion server.

## Website requests and downloads

Course and chat features communicate with JUST eLearning through your existing browser session. Resources linked by teachers may be hosted on other sites, and opening those links may contact those sites directly.

When you choose to download a resource or converted document, the extension asks your browser to start that download. The browser's own download settings still apply.

## Sharing and analytics

Rattibha Learn does not include advertising or a developer-operated analytics service. It does not sell personal information or send it to a developer-owned database. Data necessary to use eLearning is sent to the university services you already use.

## Your choices

You can remove locally saved information by clearing the extension's storage or uninstalling it. Doing so will not delete official course materials, messages or records from JUST.

## Contact

You can report privacy questions via the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Abulmeg/Rattibha-Extension) or contact the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/Abulmeg).

Rattibha Learn is not an official Jordan University of Science and Technology extension. This policy may change when features or data handling change.
